PlannerTool turns Azure DevOps work into a shared planning view for teams, schedules and capacity.

It helps teams explore work, compare scenarios and understand how plans connect. It is a planning tool, not a second work-item database.

The tool implements a comprehensive planning layer on top of Azure DevOps, providing enhanced visibility and control over project planning and execution.

What is it and what can you do

  • See work across plans, with hierarchy and dependencies in context.
  • Schedule work and compare team capacity across the timeline.
  • Explore editable scenarios, then review proposed changes before publishing.
  • Use filters and focused tools for dependencies, events, annotations and cost forecasting.

Getting into your account

  1. Use your work email. First enrollment asks for your real name; an enrolled account asks for its current account key.
  2. If Azure DevOps requires a PAT, enter it in the separate masked field. Existing account PATs stay with the account.
  3. Save the new account key shown after enrollment. It replaces the previous key and cannot be shown again.

Your PlannerTool account key and Azure PAT are different credentials. Configure or replace a PAT in Configuration, not with your account key.
